## 🇨🇳 中文

面向 A 股、港股、美股与中概股财报的深度因果分析：把收入、毛利率、费用率、现金流等指标的变化，翻译成读者能用的「为什么」。它会判断一次变动属于一次性扰动、周期波动还是公司经营结构改变，公司能否靠调价、控成本、换客户、换供应商或调整产能消解，以及接下来该盯哪些公开指标去验证。每个进入正文的数字都登记到事实表并绑定来源，最终以 [n] 标记在文末列出出处，不让印象或模糊口径混入比较。不做纯股价、估值、评级、目标价或未锚定具体报告期的宏观行业讨论；交付物是可直接阅读的分析正文，并附固定风险提示。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 输入某公司某报告期财报 → 输出可直读的因果分析正文
- 收入函数先行，沿变量逐层归因（毛利率、现金流、费用率等）
- 判断一次性扰动 / 周期波动 / 结构性变化，并如实标注置信度
- 回答「公司能否靠调价、控成本、换客户、换供应商、调产能消解」
- 正文数字登记事实表并绑定来源，文末自动生成 [n] 来源列表
- 收尾给出后续可验证的公开指标，并附固定风险提示

## 🇬🇧 English

Deep causal analysis of earnings reports for A-share, Hong Kong, US and China-concept listings: it translates moves in revenue, gross margin, expense ratio and cash flow into the "why" a reader can use. Each change is judged as a one-off disturbance, a cyclical swing or a structural shift in how the business operates, whether the company can absorb it through pricing, cost control, changing customers or suppliers, or adjusting capacity, and which public metrics to watch next for verification. Every number in the body is logged to a fact table and bound to a source, rendered as [n] markers with a source list at the end, so impressions and mixed definitions never enter a comparison. Pure share-price, valuation, rating and target-price work, and macro discussion unanchored to a specific reporting period, are out of scope. The deliverable is reader-ready analysis prose with a fixed risk disclaimer.

### ✨ What it does

- A company and reporting period in → reader-ready causal analysis prose out
- Starts from the revenue function and attributes moves variable by variable: margin, cash flow, expense ratio
- Classifies each move as one-off, cyclical or structural, with an honest confidence level
- Answers whether the company can absorb it via pricing, cost control, customers, suppliers or capacity
- Logs every body number to a fact table and binds it to a source, auto-generating the [n] source list
- Closes with public metrics that can verify the call, plus a fixed risk disclaimer
